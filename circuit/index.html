Flame sensor

Sensor pinESP32
VCC3V3
GNDGND
D0 (digital out)G15
A0not used
Use D0, not A0. The module's D0 LED lights when it sees a flame. Turn its blue screw to set the distance.

MQ-2 gas / smoke

Sensor pinESP32

MQ-2 AO ── 10 kΩ ──┬── G34

AO can reach 5 V; G34 accepts 3.3 V at most. The divider brings it down safely. Clean air reads about 260; the alarm triggers at 800. Allow 2 minutes of warm-up.

IR sensors (people counter)

  OUTSIDE  │  door  │  INSIDE
   [IR A]  │ ─────► │  [IR B]

 A then B (within 1.5 s) = entry
 B then A (within 1.5 s) = exit
Mount them 10–15 cm apart at waist height, pointing across the doorway. Adjust each blue screw so the LED lights only when someone passes.

DHT22 temperature & humidity

Sensor pinESP32
+ / VCC3V3
OUT / DATA / SG4
− / GNDGND
Wire by the labels printed on the module, since pin order differs between brands. A bare 4-pin DHT22 also needs a 10 kΩ resistor from DATA to 3V3.

Buzzer

Buzzer pinESP32
+ (long leg)G25
−GND
Use an active buzzer (it beeps by itself on 3–5 V). For a louder buzzer, drive it through an NPN transistor (2N2222 / S8050) with a 1 kΩ base resistor.
Never use G0, G2 or G12 for the buzzer: they control how the ESP32 boots.

Power & ground

RailFeeds
VIN 5 V (from USB)MQ-2 heater
3V3DHT22, flame, IR A, IR B
GNDEvery sensor and the buzzer
All grounds must be connected together. Use a short, good-quality USB data cable; the MQ-2 heater draws about 150 mA, and a weak cable causes USB dropouts.
The fan runs separately from its own 12 V adapter.
